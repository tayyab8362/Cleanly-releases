# Installing Cleanly

1. Download **Cleanly-x.y.z.dmg** (from your Gumroad receipt, or the latest version here).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Cleanly** into **Applications**.
3. Open Cleanly from Applications.

## "Cleanly can't be opened" on first launch

Cleanly is made by an independent developer and isn't yet notarized by Apple, so macOS
shows a warning the first time. This is a one-time step:

**macOS 15 Sequoia or newer**
1. Click **Done** on the warning.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to *Security*.
3. Next to *"Cleanly" was blocked…* click **Open Anyway**, then **Open**.

**macOS 13 / 14**
- Right-click (or Control-click) **Cleanly** in Applications → **Open** → **Open**.

After that it opens normally forever.

## Activate

Open Cleanly → click the broom in the menu bar → **Buy Licence…** → paste the license key
from your Gumroad receipt → **Activate**. You get 7 days to try it first.

**Seats:** the number of seats you chose on Gumroad is the number of Macs the key works on
at the same time (1 seat = 1 Mac, 4 seats = 4 Macs). Use the same key on each Mac. To move
a seat to a new Mac, open the licence window on the old one and click
**Remove licence from this Mac** first.

## Updates

Cleanly checks for updates once a day and offers them with one click. You can turn this off
in *Settings → Updates* and check manually instead.

## Full keyboard lock (optional)

By default regular keys, ⌘Tab and Force Quit are paused while you clean. To also pause
⌘Space, brightness/volume and Touch ID: *Settings → General → Grant Accessibility Access*.
Cleanly never reads what you type.
